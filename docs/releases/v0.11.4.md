# Release v0.11.4

The shared-files screen in the phone app listed the access an account was
granted alongside the links it had published. Access granted here is not
something the account shared, and it no longer appears anywhere in the app.

## The change

The share API answers from public links alone.

- **A listing reports links and nothing else.** The shared-files screen, a
  folder's sharing panel, the folder-by-folder listing under the account
  root, and "shared with you" all draw from the link store now. An access
  grant is how this deployment hands an account a share in the first place,
  and the grant table records no author, so a grant made through a client
  and one written by an administrator are the same row. There is no honest
  way to show one and hide the other, and showing both listed a person's own
  access as something they had shared out.
- **Sharing with an account or a group is refused.** It answers 403 and says
  the deployment shares by link only, rather than writing a grant the client
  would never see again. The share picker answers the shape a client needs
  to open its panel and offers nobody, and the capabilities document reports
  group sharing as off rather than on.

Access itself is unchanged. Grants are administered where they always were,
on the admin screens, and every account still reaches exactly what it did
before.

## Verification

An account is granted a folder, and every listing the app performs is checked
for it: the shared-files screen, the folder's panel, the root listing and
"shared with you". A link on the same share then lands in the screen, so the
absence is the grant and not a broken listing. Creating an account or group
share is checked for both the refusal and that no grant was written.
